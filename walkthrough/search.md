# Search and copy paths

- Type in "Search" and press Enter. Enter goes to the next match, Shift+Enter to the previous one. Keys and values are both searched, in the whole file.
- Click any node. The bar at the bottom shows its JSONPath, such as `$.items[3].id`.
- "Copy JSONPath", "Copy JS path" (`data.items[3].id`) and "Copy value" put it on the clipboard.
- Buttons 1, 2, 3 and All expand the tree to that level. Arrow keys move and open nodes.
